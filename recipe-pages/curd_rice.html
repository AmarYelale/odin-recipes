Curd Rice 🥣


Description

Curd Rice is a simple South Indian dish made with cooked rice and fresh curd. It is cooling, healthy, and perfect for summer.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 cup curd
  • Salt
  • 1 tsp butter or ghee
  • Coriander leaves (optional)

Steps

  1. Mash the cooked rice slightly.
  2. Add curd and mix well.
  3. Add salt.
  4. Mix in butter or ghee.
  5. Garnish with coriander and serve.
