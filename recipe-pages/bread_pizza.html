Bread Pizaa 🍕


Description

Bread Pizza is a quick snack made with bread, vegetables, cheese, and pizza sauce. It takes only 10 minutes and doesn't require pizza dough.

Ingredients

  • 2 bread slices
  • 2 tbsp pizza sauce
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Chopped onion
  • Chopped capsicum

Steps

  1. Spread pizza sauce on the bread.
  2. Add onion and capsicum.
  3. Sprinkle grated cheese.
  4. Toast on a pan or bake for 8-10 minutes.
  5. Serve hot.
