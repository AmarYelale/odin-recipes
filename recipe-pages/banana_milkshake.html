Banana Milkshake 🍌


Description

Banana Milkshake is a refreshing drink made with ripe bananas and chilled milk. It is perfect for breakfast or after a workout.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 cups chilled milk
  • 2 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Peel and slice the bananas.
  2. Add bananas, milk, sugar, and ice to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Pour into a glass.
  5. Serve immediately.
